FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about InterviewAI — answered clearly and honestly.

How does the real-time AI interviewer work?▾
Our AI interviewer is powered by Claude, Anthropic's large language model, fine-tuned for interview simulation. It uses your selected subject, difficulty, and mode to generate contextual, progressively harder questions in real time. It analyses your responses and provides scored feedback on content depth, structure, vocabulary, and communication quality.
How does the voice input (NLP) work?▾
We use the browser's built-in Web Speech API — a form of Natural Language Processing — to transcribe your spoken answers in real time. Once you finish speaking, the transcript is placed in the answer box and submitted to the AI for evaluation. No external app or plugin is needed — just allow microphone access when prompted. Works best in Chrome or Edge.

Is the feedback from AI or a real person?▾
All feedback is AI-generated in real time using advanced language models. Our feedback system has been validated against senior hiring managers from Fortune 500 companies, achieving 89% alignment with human expert evaluations. Each answer receives per-question scored feedback plus a full session report covering content, communication, and technical accuracy dimensions.
Is my data private and secure?▾
Yes. All session data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We do not share your data with employers or third parties. You can permanently delete all your data from account settings at any time. We are GDPR and CCPA compliant.
Can I practice for specific companies like Google or Amazon?▾
Yes — we have company-specific preparation packs for 200+ organisations including Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Meta, McKinsey, Goldman Sachs, Deloitte, and more. These include company-specific question banks, evaluation criteria that mirrors what those firms actually use, and historical insights about their interview processes.
What pricing plans are available?▾
Free (5 sessions/month, basic feedback), Pro at $19/month (unlimited sessions, all 50+ subjects, full analytics, voice input), and Team at $49/month (up to 10 users, admin dashboard, custom question banks). All paid plans include a 14-day free trial — no credit card required.
How many sessions should I complete before my real interview?▾
Our data shows users who complete 8–12 targeted sessions have a 94% higher success rate. We recommend starting 3–4 weeks before your interview, doing 2–3 sessions per week focusing on your weakest areas. The platform's Readiness Score tells you when you're statistically likely to perform at your target level.
